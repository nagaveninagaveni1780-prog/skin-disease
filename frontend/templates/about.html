About SkinSense

SkinSense is an AI-assisted skin assessment system built as a college project. It combines image-based analysis with an interactive symptom questionnaire to provide an initial, educational assessment of common skin conditions.

The system supports two modes of interaction: uploading a photo of the affected area for visual analysis, or answering a short set of questions about your symptoms through a conversational assistant.

This project is intended for educational purposes only and does not replace professional medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified dermatologist for confirmation, especially for symptoms that are severe, spreading, painful, or persistent.
